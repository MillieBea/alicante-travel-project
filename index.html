Why Alicante?

Where the Mediterranean meets everyday magic

Alicante is a city that invites you to slow down and enjoy the moment. Wander through the colourful streets of the old town, admire the views from Santa Bárbara Castle, or stroll along the palm-lined Explanada beside the Mediterranean Sea. With its golden beaches, historic landmarks and beautiful coastal scenery, the city offers something to discover around every corner.

Beyond its postcard-worthy views, Alicante has a warmth that makes everyday life feel special. Enjoy a leisurely coffee in a sunny square, savour a traditional Spanish rice dish, or watch the sunset over the harbour as the city comes alive in the evening. Whether you're visiting for a few days or staying a little longer, Alicante has a way of making you feel welcome and giving you reasons to return.

Madness - Coffee Designers

Serious about coffee, but never boring! Madness combines its own coffee roasting operation with a tempting menu of breakfast favourites and brunch classics.

Address:

📍 Calle San Nicolás, 4, 03002, Alicante

What makes it special:

Coffee gets the designer treatment here. From bean origins to the final cup, there's plenty for coffee enthusiasts to get excited about — and a very good excuse for one more coffee..

Brunchit - Coffee & Kitchen

For those who believe breakfast deserves a little extra attention, Brunchit serves speciality coffee alongside fluffy pancakes, sourdough bread and colourful breakfast bowls.

Address:

📍 Av. de la Condomina, 40, Local 21, 03540, Alicante

What makes it special:

Skipping breakfast is not an option here! Choosing what to order might be the hardest part of your morning.

Swallow - Specialty Coffee

Tucked away in Alicante's old town, Swallow is a lovely little stop for speciality coffee and a light bite between exploring historic streets and getting happily lost in the city.

Address:

📍 Plaza del Carmen, 13, 03002, Alicante

What makes it special:

Its old-town setting gives you the perfect excuse to slow down, recharge and watch the world go by. After all, sightseeing is thirsty work!
